# Roadmap

## Available in 0.1.0
- Pure analysis APIs, unit-bearing data contracts and synthetic fixtures.
- Simulated acquisition/scan orchestration and CSV/JSON/HTML output.

## Planned
- Document more non-Gaussian/noisy beam examples and fit failure cases.
- Design explicit raw-image retention and reanalysis manifests.
- Connect sample/device identifiers to PL, LIV and spectral results.
- Evaluate optional hardware adapters as separate, independently licensed integrations.
- Develop predictive baselines only after suitable traceable data is available.

These are development directions, not released capabilities or performance promises.
